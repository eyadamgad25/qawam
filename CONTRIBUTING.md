# Contributing (student project)

- Conventional commits: `feat:`, `fix:`, `docs:`, `chore:`, `test:`, `refactor:`.
- One PR-sized change per task.
- No clinical thresholds without `PLACEHOLDER_NEEDS_PHYSIO_APPROVAL` or a named physio sign-off in `docs/decisions.md`.
- No dataset files, `.env`, or real names of patients in git.
- Append experiment/decision logs when behavior or a design choice changes.
