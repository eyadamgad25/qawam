# KIMORE study — one phone camera on real patients

Generated by `ml/eval/kimore_eval.py` from KIMORE's Kinect v2 skeletons (GPP group: stroke, Parkinson's, low-back pain). Ground truth is the goniometer-style angle: the body-aligned Kinect skeleton projected orthographically onto the exercise's anatomical plane; each recording is projected into virtual phone cameras placed around the patient (0° = facing the camera, 90° = side-on), 2.5 m away at chest height. Reps are segmented on the ground-truth angle so the phone and the goniometer compare the same reps. Cue tolerance = 10° (Qawam's default). This isolates camera placement; MediaPipe's own detector error was measured on REHAB24-6.
