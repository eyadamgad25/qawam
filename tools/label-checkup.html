Check-up labelling

Mark when each test starts and ends on a stick-figure replay. The app's own result is never shown here. Labels stay in this browser until you download them.

Rules (same for every recording)
  1. Start = the first frame where the hips leave the chair (the body starts rising). Leaning forward alone is not the start.
  2. End, five-times sit-to-stand = the first frame where the hips are back down at chair level after the 5th stand-up.
  3. End, Timed Up and Go = the first frame where the hips are back down at chair level after walking back.
  4. If the recording does not show a complete test, press Cannot label.

Pick the files in data/derived/checkup/to_label/. Keys: ← → one frame, Shift+← → ten frames, Space play/pause, S start, E end.
