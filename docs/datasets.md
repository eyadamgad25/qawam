# Dataset register

Raw data never enters git (`data/raw/` is ignored). Cite each dataset in any write-up.

| Dataset | People | What it gives Qawam | Labels | Licence | Source | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| REHAB24-6 | 10 | RGB video (2 cameras), mocap 2D/3D skeletons, 6 exercises | correct / incorrect per rep | CC BY-NC 4.0 + research-only text | https://zenodo.org/records/13305826 | in use (train/val; test 7, 9 sealed) |
| REHAB26-ViewAngles | 5 | MediaPipe 2D/3D skeletons, 3 cameras x 11 orientations, squat / arm abduction / shoulder extension | correct / incorrect + error type | CC BY-NC 4.0 | https://zenodo.org/records/23018128 | in use (`ml/eval/rehab26_eval.py`, `ml/eval/quality_model.py`); 2D points of exercises 1 and 6 in `data/raw/rehab26` |
| IntelliRehabDS | 30 | Kinect v2 skeletons (25 joints), 9 gestures, standing and seated | correct / incorrect | CC BY 4.0 | https://zenodo.org/records/4610859 | in use (`ml/eval/quality_model.py`); `data/raw/SkeletonData`; person 217 left out, as the authors do |
| Rehab-Pile | 8 source datasets (KERAAL, KIMORE, IntelliRehabDS, UI-PRMD, EHE, KINECAL, SPHERE, UCDHE) | 60 ready datasets, 17,291 samples, cross-subject folds, a published benchmark of 9 models; KERAAL here is Kinect (11 joints x 7), not BlazePose | as in each source | per source dataset; code GPL-3.0 | https://msd-irimas.github.io/pages/DeepRehabPile | not downloaded |

| Fit3D | 13 | 4 synchronised RGB cameras + Vicon 3D skeletons and SMPL-X, 611 sequences, 37 fitness exercises incl. squat, at least 5 annotated reps each | repetitions; instructor as reference (no clinical labels) | non-commercial research only, single user, no training for commercial use | https://fit3d.imar.ro | training set unpacked in `data/raw/train` (8 people, 47 actions); test-set ground truth held by the challenge |
| KERAAL | 12 low-back-pain patients + 9 healthy | RGB video 480 x 360, Kinect skeletons, BlazePose (MediaPipe) and OpenPose skeletons; 3 exercises (CTK, ELK, RTK); Vicon only in group 3 (healthy, simulated errors) | per recording, from 2 clinicians (annotatorA, annotatorB): Correct / Incorrect / Incomplete / Motionless, error type, body part, small or large, and its time span | CC BY-NC-SA | http://nguyensmai.free.fr/KeraalDataset.html | groups 1A (patients, annotated) and 2A (healthy, annotated) downloaded to `data/raw/keraal/` (250 patient + 51 healthy recordings); clinicians agree on 77% of patient verdicts (kappa 0.54), see `docs/experiments.md`; loader next |
| KneE-PAD | 31 knee patients | 8 muscle (EMG) + motion (accelerometer, gyroscope) sensors; squat, seated leg extension, walking; no video in the release | correct / two wrong types per exercise | CC BY 4.0 | https://zenodo.org/records/12112951 | in use (`ml/eval/fusion_model.py`); `data/raw/knee-pad` |
| PhysioLens side leg raise | 5 healthy | MediaPipe points from a front webcam, 18 recordings, some on another day | per recording: correct, or a deliberate fault (bent knee, too shallow) | CC BY 4.0 | https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/mmujtabax/physiolens-side-leg-raise-form-dataset | used once for confirmation (`ml/eval/confirm_reference.py`); `data/raw/physiolens`; file names contain first names, never put them in results |
| Shifaa Arabic medical consultations | 55,184 distinct consultations | questions and doctors' answers in Arabic; no patient names, but doctor names and personal stories: never commit or quote texts | diagnosis category | Apache 2.0 | https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/ahmedseleem/shifaa-arabic-medical-consultations | in use (`ml/pain/shifaa_eval.py`); `data/raw/shifaa` |
| WLU rehabilitation posture | not numbered, so no split by person | 339 blurred videos: arm raise, knee extension, sit to stand | correct / incorrect by folder | MIT | https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/sulaimanmuhammed/wlu-rehabilitation-posture | `notebooks/video_to_points_kaggle.ipynb` (points are extracted on Kaggle) |

| KIMORE | 78 incl. patients | Kinect skeletons, RGB-D, 5 exercises | clinical scores | see paper | https://doi.org/10.1109/TNSRE.2019.2923060 | pending: download by hand |
| UI-PRMD | 10 | Vicon / Kinect skeletons, 10 movements | correct / incorrect | public domain | https://webpages.uidaho.edu/ui-prmd/ | pending: host not reachable from the cloud workspace |
| UCO Physical Rehabilitation (videos) | 27 | RGB multi-view video | none for quality | on request | https://github.com/AVAuco/ucophyrehab | optional: email inforeha@uco.es |

Splits are always by person. For cross-dataset tests, a model or threshold is fitted on one dataset and evaluated, untouched, on another.
